poster

A personal publishing tool

poster is a small program that runs on my own computer. It schedules short videos I make and sends them to my own social media accounts at the times I choose.

On YouTube it uploads my Shorts to my channel through YouTube API Services, with the title and description I wrote. On TikTok it uploads a video to my account's inbox, and I finish and publish the post myself in the TikTok app. On Instagram it publishes my Reels.

It is used only by its owner. It is not offered to other people and has no other users. By using its YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See the terms and privacy policy.

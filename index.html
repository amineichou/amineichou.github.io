poster

A personal publishing tool

poster is a small program that runs on my own computer. It schedules short videos I make and sends them to my own social media accounts at the times I choose. On TikTok it uploads a video to my account's inbox, and I finish and publish the post myself in the TikTok app.

It is used only by its owner. It is not offered to other people and has no other users.

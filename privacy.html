Privacy policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

What data poster uses

When the owner logs in to TikTok through poster, TikTok returns an access token and the account's open ID (scopes user.info.basic and video.upload). poster uses them only to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own account.

Where it is stored

The token and a list of uploaded videos (file name, upload time and the ID TikTok returns) are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than the platforms' own APIs.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or used for advertising or analytics.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok's settings (Security and permissions, Apps) and delete poster's local files.

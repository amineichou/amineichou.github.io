Privacy policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026 (Facebook added)

poster is a personal tool that runs on its owner's computer and uploads the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook accounts. This policy explains what it uses and keeps.

YouTube API Services

poster uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to YouTube. By using poster's YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What poster accesses and keeps

  • YouTube: when the owner signs in with Google, poster receives an OAuth token for the youtube.upload permission. It uses it only to upload the owner's videos to the owner's channel (the videos.insert method), with the title and description the owner wrote. It does not read channel data, analytics, comments, subscribers or anyone else's data. For each upload it keeps the video ID and link that YouTube returns.
  • TikTok: an access token and the account's open ID (permissions user.info.basic and video.upload), used only to send the owner's videos to the owner's TikTok inbox, and the upload ID TikTok returns.
  • Instagram: an access token, the account's user ID and username, used only to publish the owner's Reels, and the post ID and link Instagram returns.
  • Facebook: an access token for the owner's own Facebook Page (permissions pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement and pages_manage_posts), the Page's ID and name, used only to publish the owner's Reels on that Page, and the video ID Facebook returns.

How it is stored and used

Tokens and the list of uploads (file name, time, title or caption, and the ID and link each platform returns) are stored only on the owner's computer, in files readable only by the owner. They are used only to post the owner's videos on schedule and to show the owner what has been posted.

Sharing

poster does not sell, share or transfer any data, does not show ads or third-party content, and has no analytics. Data goes only to the platforms' own APIs. If the owner turns on phone notifications, a short message (the clip's name, its caption and the post's link) is sent to the owner's own phone through the ntfy.sh notification service.

Cookies and device storage

poster does not use cookies or tracking. Its dashboard runs on the owner's computer and keeps view preferences (such as filters) in that browser's local storage. This website sets no cookies.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time: for YouTube in the Google security settings page, for TikTok in the app under Settings, Security and permissions, Apps, for Instagram under Settings, Apps and websites, and for Facebook under Settings, Business integrations (or Apps and websites). After access is revoked, poster's stored token and upload records for that account are deleted within 7 days. To ask for any stored data to be deleted, email the address below; it is deleted within 7 days.
